Any other use needs a separate commercial license from the copyright holder, for
example:

* using hashstash in, or to develop, a product or service that is sold or earns money
* using it inside a company or other business, including for internal tools
* hosting it as a service for others
* building it into, or shipping it with, a commercial product

To ask about a commercial license, open an issue at
<https://github.com/richapma78/hashstash/issues> with the title "Commercial license",
or contact the owner through <https://github.com/richapma78>. If you're not sure
whether your use counts as commercial, ask.

## Third-party code

hashstash builds on libraries that keep their own terms: tree-sitter and its
grammars, BLAKE3, cpp-httplib and nlohmann/json under permissive licenses, and
SQLite, which is public domain (see `cmake/deps.cmake`). This license covers hashstash's own code, not
theirs.
